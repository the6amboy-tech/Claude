# Asharas brand essentials

## Brand idea

Asharas makes music discovery feel personal, immediate, and shared. The identity combines a clear “A” with a play-shaped counter: the brand name and the act of listening in one compact mark.

## Logo system

- Primary mark: `assets/logos/asharas-mark.svg`
- Horizontal wordmark: `assets/logos/asharas-wordmark.svg`
- Maskable app source: `assets/logos/asharas-maskable.svg`
- Social preview source: `assets/social/asharas-social-card.svg`
- Search/browser favicons: `favicon.ico`, `favicon.svg`, `favicon-48.png`, `favicon-64.png`, and `favicon-32.png`
- Minimum digital size: 24px for the mark and 120px for the wordmark.
- Clear space: keep at least one quarter of the mark width around the logo.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor, outline, or add effects to the logo artwork.

## Palette

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asharas Red | `#FF375F` | Logo, primary actions, active states |
| Midnight | `#08080B` | Primary dark background |
| Frost | `#F5F5F7` | Primary text and reversed mark |
| Mist | `#AAAAB3` | Secondary text |
| Graphite Glass | `rgba(31,31,36,.64)` | Translucent surfaces |

## Typography and voice

Use the system San Francisco stack already present in the product. Headlines are direct and warm; interface copy is calm, concise, and human. The preferred public line is “Music, in sync.”
